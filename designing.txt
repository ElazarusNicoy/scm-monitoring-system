• Identify process stage and define SLA thresholds for each transaction.

    Request Check Preparation (RCP)
    Normal: pending for less than 15 days
    Warning: pending for 15 days to 20 days
    Critical: pending for 21 days or more

    Purchase Requisition (PR)
    Normal: pending for less than 15 days
    Warning: pending for 15 days to 20 days
    Critical: pending for 21 days or more


    Movement Approval Sheet (MAS)
    Normal: pending for less than 5 days
    Warning: pending for 5 days to 10 days
    Critical: pending for 11 days or more


    Purchase Order Amendment/Cancellation Request
    Normal: pending for less than 5 days
    Warning: pending for 5 days to 10 days
    Critical: pending for 11 days or more

    Work Order Amendment Form
    Normal: pending for less than 5 days
    Warning: pending for 5 days to 10 days
    Critical: pending for 11 days or more
